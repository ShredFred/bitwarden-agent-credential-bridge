# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive place to discuss security
engineering. Maintainers and contributors commit to participating without
harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while challenging ideas rigorously.
- Keep criticism technical, specific, and focused on the work.
- Protect privacy. Do not share credentials, private data, personal information,
  or security-sensitive details that do not belong in public.
- Help newcomers understand the project's unusually strict safety boundaries.
- Use Discussions, Issues, Pull Requests, and private vulnerability reporting
  for their intended purposes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal insults, bad-faith attacks, or publishing another person's private
  information.
- Posting live credentials, recovery codes, private endpoints, or exploit
  details in public channels.
- Deliberately pressuring contributors to bypass the project's fake-only,
  fail-closed, or approval-gated boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, lock conversations, or restrict participation
when behavior violates this code of conduct. Report public conduct concerns
through GitHub's reporting controls or a verified private maintainer channel.
Potential security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md instead.
